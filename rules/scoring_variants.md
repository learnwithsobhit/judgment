# Scoring variants

**rule_id:** `scoring-variants-001`  
**ruleset_version:** `mvp-1`  
**category:** scoring

MVP ships **exact-bid** scoring only (`scoring-exact-001`). Other regional
variants (overtrick bonuses, undertrick penalties, nil specials) are documented
here for future rooms and must not be assumed by the assistant unless the
active room config selects them.
