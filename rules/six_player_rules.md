# Multi-player seat rules (3–8)

**rule_id:** `multi-player-001`  
**ruleset_version:** `mvp-1`  
**category:** gameplay

Rooms support **3 to 8** seated players. Card schedules and undealt cards scale
with seat count. Follow-suit, trump, bidding order, and exact-bid scoring are
unchanged; only the number of hands and the deal depth differ.
