# Basic gameplay

**rule_id:** `basic-gameplay-001`  
**ruleset_version:** `mvp-1`  
**category:** gameplay

Judgement (also known as Oh Hell) is a trick-taking card game. Each round every
player secretly bids how many tricks they will win, then tries to win **exactly**
that many.

## Deck and seats

- Standard 52-card deck, Ace high within a suit.
- 3–8 players sit clockwise around the table.
- Play and bidding always proceed clockwise.

## A round in short

1. Dealer deals the round’s cards.
2. Trump is decided (revealed undealt card, or a chosen suit that rotates).
3. Players bid, starting left of the dealer; the dealer bids last.
4. Tricks are played; you must follow the lead suit if you can.
5. Exact bids score; misses score zero.
6. The dealer rotates clockwise for the next round.
