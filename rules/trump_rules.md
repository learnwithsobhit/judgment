# Trump rules

**rule_id:** `trump-001`  
**ruleset_version:** `mvp-1`  
**category:** trump

Trump outranks every other suit for the purpose of winning a trick.

## Revealed undealt card (default)

After the deal, one undealt card is revealed. Its suit becomes trump for the round.
If every card was dealt (no undealt card remains), the round has **no trump**.

## Chosen first trump + suit rotation

Rooms may instead let the first dealer **choose** the opening trump suit. On later
rounds the trump suit **rotates** in a fixed order (for example Clubs → Diamonds →
Hearts → Spades → Clubs…). Rotation continues for the whole match unless the room
rules say otherwise.

## Playing trump

- You may play trump only when you cannot follow the lead suit, or when trump is
  the lead suit.
- When trump is led, every player who holds trump must follow with trump.
- Among cards that can win, the **highest trump** always beats any non-trump.
